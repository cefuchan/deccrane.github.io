DEC ANCHOR WINDLASS WINCH

DEC Anchor Windlass Winches are designed to provide safe, efficient, and reliable anchoring operations for various types of vessels. Built for durability and high performance, these winches ensure smooth and controlled anchor handling, even in the harshest marine environments.

KEY FEATURES

✅ Drive System: Available in Hydraulic or Electric Drive
✅ Chainlifter Configuration: Single or Double Chainlifter options
✅ Drum Configuration: Single or Double Drum options
✅ Warping End Configuration: Available in Single or Double Warping End
✅ Chain Size Compatibility: Suitable for chain sizes ranging from 12mm to 81mm
✅ Rated Pull: Designed according to class regulations
✅ Clutch System: Manual operated clutch for enhanced control
✅ Brake System: Manual operated band brake for secure stopping
✅ Control System: Local control for easy and safe operation

OPTIONAL FEATURES

🔹 Mooring Drum: Available with or without a mooring drum
🔹 Warping End: Configurable with or without warping end
🔹 Remote Operation: Wireless remote control for increased flexibility
🔹 Hydraulic Brake System: Optional hydraulic-operated brake for enhanced braking efficiency
🔹 Bridge Control: Bridge-operated anchor drop for centralized vessel operation
🔹 Chain Monitoring: Chain length and speed measurement for precise control
🔹 Chain Stoppers: Available with chain stopper(s) for additional security

With its high-quality construction and advanced features, DEC Anchor Windlass Winches offer superior performance and long-term reliability, making them the ideal choice for commercial vessels, offshore platforms, and naval applications.
