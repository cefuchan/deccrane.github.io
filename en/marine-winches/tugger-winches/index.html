DEC TUGGER WINCHES

DEC CRANE offers a wide range of electric and hydraulic-driven tugger winches, designed for heavy-duty lifting and pulling in marine and offshore applications.

KEY FEATURES

✅ Hydraulic or Electric Drive
✅ Single drum with/without warping end configuration
✅ Rated Pull (1st Layer): 5T - 30T
✅ Drum Capacity: 50m - 600m steel wire rope
✅ Manually operated band brake
✅ Manually operated clutch
✅ Local control

OPTIONAL FEATURES

🔹 With or without warping end
🔹 Hydraulic  clutch
🔹 Hydraulic band brake
🔹 Wireless remote control
🔹 Additional options available upon request to meet specific operational needs
