DEC MOORING CAPSTANS

DEC offers a wide range of mooring capstans designed to ensure safe and efficient mooring operations for various vessel types. Our capstans are available in deck-mounted (pedestal) and underdeck (flush-mounted) configurations, providing flexible solutions based on operational needs.

KEY FEATURES

✅ Drive Options: Electric or hydraulic drive systems for reliable performance.
✅ Installation Types: Available as deck-mounted (pedestal) or underdeck (flush-mounted) for optimized space usage.
✅ Pulling Capacity: Rated pull ranging from 1 to 50 tons, ensuring secure mooring operations.
✅ Speed Options: Single-speed, two-speed, or variable-speed configurations.
✅ Durability: Constructed from high-strength marine-grade materials for maximum corrosion resistance and longevity.

OPTIONAL FEATURES

✔ Warping End: Available with or without warping end, depending on mooring needs.
✔ Chainlifter Option: Can be supplied with or without a chainlifter for anchor chain handling.
✔ Fail-Safe Brake System: Hydraulic or electric braking for added safety.
✔ Remote Control: Optional remote control for enhanced operational flexibility.
✔ Customized Configurations: Tailored solutions based on vessel specifications and operational requirements.

DEC’s mooring capstans are designed to meet international maritime standards, offering robust and efficient solutions for ship mooring. For custom requirements, contact our technical team for expert guidance.
