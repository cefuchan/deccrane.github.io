DEC ACTIVE HEAVE COMPENSATION (AHC) WINCHES

DEC AHC Winch is an advanced system designed for precise and stable load handling from vessels or rigs to underwater targets such as the seabed, subsea installations, or other fixed structures. This winch is equipped with Active Heave Compensation (AHC) technology, ensuring that the load remains at a constant elevation regardless of the vertical movements of the vessel or rig caused by waves, wind, or other environmental factors.

The AHC system utilizes real-time data from a Motion Reference Unit (MRU) to track the vessel’s motion and determine the relative position of the load. Based on this input, the DEC AHC Winch automatically adjusts the cable tension, either paying in or paying out, to maintain the load’s position without any vertical displacement.

✅ SWL (Safe Working Load): From 5 tons to 50 tons
✅ Wire Length: Up to 4,500 meters
✅ Closed-loop Hydraulic Drive
✅ Drum Type: Options for grooved or Lebus drum
✅ Hydraulic Operated Band Brakes
✅ Auto-mechanic Spooling Device to manage the wire effectively during operations
✅ Constant Tension
✅ Motion Reference Unit (MRU) to sense movements of the vessel, caused by wave action, related to the seabed.
✅ Spooling and Fleeting Systems: Auto-mechanic spooling system or automatic fleeting system to manage the wire effectively during operation.
✅ Control Unit and HPU in closed Container
✅ Control Options: Local and/or remote control
✅ Online remote service and data download access
✅ A-frame
