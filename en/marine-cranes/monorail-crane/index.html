DEC MONORAIL CRANE

 DEC CRANE Telescopic Monorail Crane is designed to provide efficent, precise and flexible lifting solutions for marine enviroments, specifically for handling materials from outside the vessel. The crane extends outward from ship's side,allowing for lifting operations even when the vessel is docked or operating in restricted spaces. 

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

✔ Safe Working Load (SWL): 1 – 5 tons
✔ Outreach: 1 – 6 meters
✔ Telescopic Extension: Extends beyond the ship's side for external load handling
✔ Mounting:Ceiling-mounted, Ensuring maximum space efficency

✔ Smooth and Balanced Movement with the rach gear system 
✔ Operated from a remote control or local control panel
✔ Forward/Backward movement, extension, and retraction are fully contollable

The DEC CRANE Telescopic Monorail Crane offers a reliable and stable solution for external lifting operations, even in challenging marine conditions. Contact us today for further details or customizations for your specific lifting needs.
