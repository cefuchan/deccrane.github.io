DEC HOSE HANDLING CRANE

The DEC Crane Hose Handling Crane is a specialized deck crane designed for the safe and efficient handling of hoses at oil cargo manifolds and bunker connections on tankers. Manufactured in compliance with OCIMF requirements, these cranes can be certified by any recognized Classification Society.

Explosion-Proof & Marine-Grade Design:

DEC Hose Handling Cranes are specifically designed to operate in hazardous areas and are powered by an explosion-proof electric motor. The electric starter is safely positioned away from hazardous zones, while EExde start/stop buttons are mounted directly on the crane for secure and efficient operation. Built to withstand the harsh marine environment, DEC cranes ensure durability, reliability, and long-lasting performance.

KEY FEATURES

✅ Explosion-proof design for hazardous environments
✅ Electro-hydraulic drive system for reliable performance
✅ Safe Working Load (SWL): Ranging from 1T to 15T
✅ Max Outreach: From 6m up to 35m
✅ Unlimited 360-degree slewing for maximum flexibility
✅ Fail-safe brakes on all crane movements
✅ Operator control platform for enhanced safety and ease of operation
✅ Standard pedestal height ready for mounting and welding to the vessel’s main steel structure
✅ Stepless speed control for all crane motions, including hoisting and lowering of the winch
✅ Automatic hook stop in the top position for safety
✅ Electric starter equipment positioned in a safe area
✅ Hand-operated pump for emergency release of the winch brake and slewing gear

OPTIONAL FEATURES

🔹 3m chain pennant
🔹 Automatic hook stop in both top and bottom positions
🔹 Portable explosion-proof electric remote control (wired or wireless)
🔹 Increased pedestal height for specific installation requirements
🔹 Limit switches for luffing, slewing, and hoisting operations
🔹 Electrical slip ring for connecting various electrical and electronic equipment
🔹 Load indicator for enhanced safety and monitoring
🔹 Enclosed operator cabin for added protection and comfort
🔹 Oil cooler for operation in hot climates
🔹 Stainless steel hydraulic piping for durability and corrosion resistance
