DEC YACHT TENDER CRANE

DEC Yacht Tender Cranes are designed to provide safe, efficient and spacsaving lifting solutions for yachts, mega yachts and luxury vessels. These Cranes offer precise handling of tenders, jet skis, and other watercraft with a compact sleek design, making them an ideal choice for higend marine applications.

✅ Boom Type Options: Available in

·        Stiff Boom

·        Knuckle Boom

·       Telescopic Boom

✅ Voltage Compatibility: Operates on 12V, 24V, 230V, 380V, and 440V, ensuring flexibility across different yacht power systems
✅ Safe Working Load (SWL):From 1000 lb (454 kg) to 22050 lb (10000 kg), capable of lifting full rated capacity in any position without restriction
✅ Max Outreach:from 3m(118’’) to 10m(394’’)
✅ Slewing Sector:Unlimited 360° rotation, providing full maneuverability
✅ Hydraulic Linear Winch System:
• Standard with integrated lift overload protection
• Non-fouling, silent operating winch design
✅ Constant Payout System: Hydraulic linear winch moves with the telescoping boom extension, ensuring consistent cable payout for superior control and ease of operation
✅ Hydraulic Boom Luffing: Capable of full rated capacity at any boom angle
✅ Hydraulic Cylinders: Integrated safety valves for unmatched dependability and safe operation
✅ Electro-Hydraulic Drive System: For quiet and efficient operation, ensuring minimal impact on onboard comfort
✅ Proportional Control System:precise and smooth movements, allowing operators to fine-tune crane speed and positioning for delicate operations
✅ Foldable and Telescopic Boom: Reduces stowage space when not in use, making it ideal for compact yacht environments
✅ Emergency Hand Pump: Ensures operation in case of power failure
✅ Wireless Remote Control: Allows for easy and safe operation from a distance
