DEC TENDER DAVITS

DEC CRANE offers lightweight and durable tender davits designed for sailing boats, ensuring the safe and efficient lifting of tenders and dinghies. Manufactured from high-quality marine-grade stainless steel, these davits provide excellent corrosion resistance and long-lasting performance in harsh marine environments.

KEY FEATURES

✅ Adjustable sliding arm extension for precise positioning of the raised dinghy or tender.
✅ Lightweight Construction: Designed for easy handling and minimal impact on vessel stability.
✅ Drive Options: Available in hydraulic or electric operation for effortless lifting.
✅ Lifting Capacity: From 150 kg to 600 kg, accommodating various tender sizes.
✅ Stainless Steel Build: Provides superior durability, corrosion resistance, and strength.
✅ Optional Painted Finish: Can be customized to match yacht aesthetics.
