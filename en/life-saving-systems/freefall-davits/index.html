DEC FREEFALL DAVITS

DECFreefall Davits are designed to provide reliable and safe launching of lifeboats in emergency situations. These davits are highly durable, corrosion-resistant, and designed for operation in harsh offshore environments. They comply with all necessary maritime regulations and standards, ensuring safe and efficient deployment of lifeboats during emergencies.

STANDARD FEATURES

✔MED (Marine Equipment Directive) Certified

✔SWL: 55 kN (Standard) – Safe Working Load of 55 kN to handle the launch and recovery of lifeboats.

✔Proven Design – Reliable and cost-effective design for long-lasting performance.

✔Hydraulic Drive – Ensures smooth operation for both launching and recovery.

✔Compatible with All Lifeboat Brands – Designed to accommodate various types of lifeboats.

✔Freefall Launching – Designed for freefall launching of lifeboats, ensuring quick and safe operation.

✔Hydraulic Recovery – Efficient hydraulic system for recovery of lifeboats after use.

✔SOLAS, IMO, and LSA Compliant – Fully compliant with the latest Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS), International Maritime Organization (IMO), and Life Saving Appliance (LSA) codes.

✔Designed for Offshore Environments – Built for operation in the harsh conditions of offshore environments, ensuring reliable performance in all sea states.

✔Assembly and Function Testing – Davits can be delivered fully assembled and tested to reduce yard work and ensure quick installation.

✔Long Lifespan – Designed for durability and longevity, especially in harsh marine conditions.

✔Global Service Availability – DEC Davits’ authorized services are available in over 20 countries worldwide, ensuring global support and service.

✔Low Type Available – Low type models are available for ships with height restrictions or installation challenges.

✔Short Delivery Time – Fast delivery for urgent requirements and quick installation.

DEC Freefall Davits offer a reliable, efficient, and safe solution for lifeboat launching and recovery, meeting all necessary regulations while providing ease of installation, operation, and long-lasting performance.

🔹 Custom Modifications – Available on request to meet specific requirements or vessel specifications. Winterization Package – For use in colder climates or extreme conditions.
