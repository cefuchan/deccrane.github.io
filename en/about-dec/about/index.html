ABOUT DEC(DECK EQUIPMENTS&CRANES) CRANE

With over 55 years of experience, DEC CRANE is a leading manufacturer of high-quality marine cranes, winches and ship equipments. We specialize in providing innovative and reliable lifting solutions for maritime industry, working closely with all major classification societies(IACS members) to ensure the highest  standards of safety and performance.

Our extensive product range includes:
✅ Marine Cranes – Stiff boom, knuckle boom, telescopic boom, hose handling, provision, yacht tender and gantry cranes 
✅ Winches – Anchor windlasses, Towing Winches, Mooring winches, Tugger winches and AHC winches
✅ Tunnel Thrusters – High-efficency thrusters for maneuvering and station-keeping
✅ Freefall Davits & Rescue Boat Cranes – Designed for fast and safe launching in emergency situations

DEC also provides training, technical support, spare parts and after-sales service to ensure continous and safe operation of our equipment.

At DEC CRANE, we are comitted to engineering excellence, delivering customized solutions to meet the unique needs of our clients worldwide. Our dedication to quality, safety, and innovation has made us a trusted partner in the global maritime industry.

For more information, feel free to contact our expert team.
